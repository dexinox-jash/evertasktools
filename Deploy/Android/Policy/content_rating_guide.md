# Google Play Content Rating Guide

EverTask is a productivity/task-management app with no sensitive content. This guide gives you the exact answers for the IKO (International Age Rating Coalition) questionnaire in Google Play Console.

---

## Expected Rating

| System | Rating |
|--------|--------|
| PEGI | 3 |
| ESRB | E (Everyone) |
| IARC | Everyone |

---

## Questionnaire Answers

### Violence
- **Does the app contain violence?** → **No**
- **Does the app contain realistic violence?** → **No**
- **Does the app contain fantasy violence?** → **No**
- **Does the app contain fear or horror themes?** → **No**

### Sexual Content
- **Does the app contain sexual content or nudity?** → **No**
- **Does the app contain sexual innuendo?** → **No**

### Profanity
- **Does the app contain profanity?** → **No**

### Drugs
- **Does the app reference or depict the use of drugs, alcohol, or tobacco?** → **No**

### Gambling
- **Does the app contain gambling or simulated gambling?** → **No**

### In-App Purchases
- **Does the app offer in-app purchases?** → **No** (currently)
- If you add monetization later, update this answer.

### User Interaction
- **Does the app allow users to interact or communicate with each other?** → **No**
- **Does the app share user-generated content?** → **No**

### Location Sharing
- **Does the app share the user's precise location with other users?** → **No**

---

## App Description for Content Rating

If Play Console asks for a brief description to help with rating:

> EverTask is a local-first productivity app that helps users break down tasks into subtasks using voice input, smart templates, and reminders. The app contains no user-generated content sharing, no social features, no violence, no sexual content, no profanity, and no references to drugs, alcohol, or gambling.

---

## After You Complete the Form

Once you answer all questions as above, the Play Console will calculate:
- **PEGI 3**
- **ESRB E (Everyone)**
- **IARC Everyone**

Save the rating certificate. You will need to confirm it for every major update.
