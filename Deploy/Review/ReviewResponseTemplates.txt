Review Response Templates

Use these templates when responding to user reviews on Google Play.

---

Template 1: Positive Review (5 stars)
---
Thank you so much for the kind words! 🎉 We're thrilled that EverTask is helping you stay organized. If you have any feature requests, feel free to reach out at support@evertask.app.

---

Template 2: Bug Report / Crash
---
Sorry to hear you're experiencing an issue. We'd love to help fix it ASAP. Please email us at support@evertask.app with your device model and Android version, and we'll investigate right away.

---

Template 3: Feature Request
---
Thanks for the suggestion! We’re always looking for ways to improve EverTask. Please send your idea to support@evertask.app — we read every message and prioritize based on user feedback.

---

Template 4: Voice Recognition Issue
---
Sorry the voice input isn't working perfectly for you. Voice recognition relies on your device's built-in speech engine. Make sure you're connected to the internet (for cloud-based recognition) and that microphone permissions are granted in Settings > Apps > EverTask > Permissions.

---

Template 5: Notification / Reminder Issue
---
If you're not receiving reminders, please check two things: (1) notification permissions are allowed, and (2) on Android 12+, "Alarms & reminders" permission is enabled in Settings > Apps > EverTask. If issues persist, contact us at support@evertask.app.

---

Template 6: Widget Not Updating
---
If your home screen widget looks outdated, try removing it and re-adding it. Widgets update automatically after task changes, but some launchers cache aggressively. For persistent issues, reach out at support@evertask.app.

---

Template 7: General Negative Review (1-3 stars, vague)
---
We're sorry EverTask didn't meet your expectations. We'd really appreciate the chance to make it right — please email us at support@evertask.app and let us know what went wrong.

---

Tips for Responding
- Always thank the user, even on negative reviews.
- Keep responses under 350 characters when possible.
- Never argue or get defensive.
- Direct complex issues to email support.
- Respond within 24-48 hours for best user sentiment.
