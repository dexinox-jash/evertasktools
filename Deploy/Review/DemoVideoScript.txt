EverTask — 30-Second Promo Video Script

---

[0:00-0:03] OPENING SHOT
Show the EverTask app icon launching into the main screen.
Voiceover: "Overwhelmed by big tasks?"

[0:03-0:08] VOICE INPUT
Show the user tapping the mic button and saying, "Plan my workout."
The app instantly creates a task with subtasks: Warm up, Lift weights, Stretch, Cool down.
Voiceover: "Just say it. EverTask breaks it down into steps."

[0:08-0:14] SMART TEMPLATES
Show a few quick task creations: "Grocery shop," "Write email," "Study session."
Each automatically expands into relevant subtasks.
Voiceover: "Smart templates recognize what you need and build the plan for you."

[0:14-0:20] PROGRESS & COMPLETION
Show subtasks being checked off, the progress bar filling up, and the celebration screen with confetti.
Voiceover: "Track your progress and celebrate every win."

[0:20-0:26] WIDGETS & REMINDERS
Show the home screen widget updating in real time, then a reminder notification appearing.
Voiceover: "Widgets and reminders keep you on track without opening the app."

[0:26-0:30] CLOSING
Show the app logo and Google Play badge.
Voiceover: "EverTask. Break down any task in seconds. Download free on Google Play."

---

Visual Style Notes:
- Use the teal and white color palette from the app
- Keep transitions fast and fluid
- Show real app UI, no mockups
- Background music: upbeat, minimal, no lyrics
