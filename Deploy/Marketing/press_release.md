# Press Release: EverTask Launches on Google Play

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## EverTask — Voice-Powered Task Breakdown App Now Available on Google Play

**[City, State] — [Date]** — EverTask, a local-first productivity app that transforms overwhelming to-do lists into simple, actionable steps using voice commands and smart templates, is now available for free on the Google Play Store.

### Break Down Any Task in Seconds

Traditional to-do apps force users to stare at a blank screen and manually organize every step. EverTask eliminates that friction. Users simply say a task out loud — such as "Plan my workout" or "Clean my room" — and EverTask instantly generates a structured checklist with time estimates and progress tracking.

### Key Features

- **Voice-First Task Creation:** Speak naturally to create tasks without typing.
- **Smart Templates:** 76 built-in templates automatically break down common activities like grocery shopping, studying, cooking, and travel packing.
- **Visual Progress Tracking:** Live progress bars and a confetti celebration screen when all subtasks are complete.
- **Exact-Alarm Reminders:** Reliable local reminders so users never miss a step.
- **Home Screen Widgets:** Four Jetpack Glance widget sizes for at-a-glance task updates and one-tap completion.
- **Privacy-First:** All data stays on the device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no data collection.

### Built for Busy People

EverTask is designed for students, professionals, parents, and anyone who wants to spend less time organizing and more time doing. The app works entirely offline, making it ideal for users who value privacy and speed.

### Availability

EverTask is available now as a free download on Google Play for Android devices running Android 8.0 (API 26) and above.
