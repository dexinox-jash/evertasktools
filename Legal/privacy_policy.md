# Privacy Policy for EverTask

**Effective Date:** April 12, 2026  
**Last Updated:** April 12, 2026

## Introduction

Welcome to EverTask ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience using our task management application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the EverTask mobile application (the "App").

EverTask is designed with privacy at its core. We believe that your personal data belongs to you, and we have built our application to minimize data collection and maximize your control over your information.

## Our Privacy-First Approach

EverTask operates on a fundamental principle: **your data stays on your device**. Unlike most modern applications, EverTask does not require user accounts, does not collect personal information, and does not transmit your data to our servers or any third parties. Your tasks, notes, and personal information remain exclusively on your device unless you explicitly choose to export or back up your data.

## Information We Do Not Collect

To be absolutely clear about our privacy practices, EverTask does **NOT** collect:

- **Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.
- **User Accounts:** No account registration or login is required to use EverTask.
- **Device Information:** We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or hardware information.
- **Usage Analytics:** We do not track how you use the app, which features you access, or how long you use the application.
- **Location Data:** We do not access or collect your geographical location.
- **Contacts:** We do not access your device's contact list or address book.
- **Photos or Media:** We only access photos when you explicitly choose to attach them to a task, and these remain stored locally on your device.
- **Browsing History:** We do not monitor or collect any browsing data.
- **Third-Party Data:** We do not receive data from third-party services or partners.

## Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The only "data collection" that occurs is the information you voluntarily enter into the App to manage your tasks. This includes:

- **Task Information:** Task titles, descriptions, due dates, priorities, and completion status
- **Notes and Comments:** Any notes or comments you add to tasks
- **Categories and Lists:** Task categories, lists, and organizational structures you create
- **Attachments:** Photos, documents, or other files you explicitly attach to tasks
- **App Preferences:** Your in-app settings and preferences (such as theme selection, notification preferences, and default views)

**Important:** All of this information is stored **exclusively on your device** using local storage mechanisms. We do not have access to this data, and it never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

## How We Use Your Information

Since all data remains on your device, our "use" of your information is limited to:

1. **Providing Core Functionality:** Displaying your tasks, managing your lists, and enabling the app's features you interact with
2. **Local Processing:** Sorting, filtering, and organizing your tasks based on your preferences and actions
3. **Notifications:** Sending local device notifications based on due dates and reminders you set
4. **Data Persistence:** Saving your data between app sessions so your information is available when you return

We do not process your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any commercial purposes.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only

EverTask uses your device's built-in storage systems to save your data. This means:

- Your data is physically stored on your device's internal memory
- No internet connection is required to access your tasks
- Your data is not synchronized with any cloud services we operate
- We cannot access, view, or modify your data under any circumstances

### Security Measures

While your data remains on your device, we have implemented security measures within the App:

- **Local Database Encryption:** The local database used by EverTask employs industry-standard encryption to protect your data from unauthorized access by other applications
- **Secure Data Handling:** We follow secure coding practices to prevent data leakage or unauthorized access
- **No Network Transmission:** By design, the app does not transmit your task data over networks, eliminating a significant attack vector

### Your Device's Security

The security of your data also depends on the security measures you implement on your device:

- We strongly recommend using a device passcode, PIN, or biometric lock
- Keep your device's operating system updated with the latest security patches
- Use device-level encryption if available on your platform

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with any third parties. Since we do not collect your data, there is nothing for us to share.

### Legal Requirements

While we maintain a strict no-collection policy, we may be required to disclose information if legally compelled. However, given that we do not possess user data (as it remains solely on users' devices), we would not have any user data to provide in response to legal requests.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have specific rights regarding your personal data under various privacy regulations.

### GDPR Rights (European Union Users)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) grants you the following rights:

1. **Right to Access:** You have the right to request copies of your personal data. Since EverTask stores all data locally on your device, you can access all your data directly through the app.

2. **Right to Rectification:** You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal data. You can edit or modify any of your task data directly within the app.

3. **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. You can delete individual tasks, lists, or all app data through your device's settings.

4. **Right to Restrict Processing:** You can limit how the app processes your data by adjusting app permissions in your device settings.

5. **Right to Data Portability:** You have the right to request your data in a structured, commonly used format. EverTask provides export functionality to allow you to extract your data.

6. **Right to Object:** You have the right to object to processing of your personal data.

7. **Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making:** EverTask does not engage in automated decision-making or profiling.

### CCPA Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you the following rights:

1. **Right to Know:** You have the right to know what personal information is collected about you. EverTask collects no personal information beyond what you voluntarily enter for task management.

2. **Right to Delete:** You have the right to request deletion of your personal information. You can delete all app data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. **Right to Opt-Out:** You have the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information. EverTask does not and will never sell any personal information.

4. **Right to Non-Discrimination:** You have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

### Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights:
- Access, modify, or delete data directly within the EverTask app
- Use the export functionality to obtain a copy of your data
- Delete the app to remove all associated data from your device
- Contact us using the information provided in the "Contact Us" section

## Children's Privacy

### COPPA Compliance (Children Under 13)

EverTask complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since our app requires no registration and collects no personal data, children can safely use EverTask for task management without privacy concerns.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately. Since all data is stored locally on the device, you can remove any information by deleting the app or clearing the app data through your device settings.

### Parental Guidance

While EverTask is safe for children to use, we recommend parental guidance for children under 13 to ensure appropriate use of the application.

## Data Backups and Exports

### Optional Encrypted Backups

EverTask offers optional backup functionality that allows you to:

- Export your data to encrypted files stored on your device
- Create backups in formats that can be imported back into EverTask
- Transfer your data to a new device

**Important Notes About Backups:**
- Backups are encrypted using industry-standard encryption algorithms
- Backup files are stored locally unless you choose to transfer them using your device's sharing capabilities
- You control where backup files are stored and who has access to them
- We do not have access to your backup files

### Third-Party Cloud Services

If you choose to use your device's built-in cloud backup services (such as iCloud for iOS or Google Drive for Android), your EverTask data may be included in those backups according to your device settings and the respective cloud service's privacy policy. This is entirely under your control and subject to those third-party services' policies, not ours.

## International Data Transfers

Since EverTask does not transmit your data to our servers, there are no international data transfers initiated by us. Your data remains on your device in your physical location. Any data export or backup you perform is entirely under your control.
